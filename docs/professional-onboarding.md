# Professional onboarding

The Studio entry point opens a nine-step, server-backed setup checklist. Completion is calculated from persisted account, business, location, portfolio, team, catalogue, pricing, availability and approval data.

Approval submission remains locked until the first eight steps are complete. Pending, approved and rejected states are shown from the business approval record; a rejection reason is returned to the owner so they can make targeted changes and resubmit.
