# Decision-ready salon cards

Discovery cards expose the information a customer needs for an initial decision without opening every profile:

- the first active portfolio image, or a clearly labelled category fallback;
- `Demo salon` for seeded examples and `Approved business` for a live listing that passed administrator approval;
- average rating with its review count, or an explicit no-reviews-yet state;
- starting price, up to three service categories and suburb;
- distance only after the customer provides coordinates;
- the next available date when the availability engine can determine one, otherwise a prompt to check appointment times.

`Approved business` describes Glow's listing approval state. It is not a guarantee of service quality, identity, licensing or outcome. More detailed trust explanations remain part of UX-303.
